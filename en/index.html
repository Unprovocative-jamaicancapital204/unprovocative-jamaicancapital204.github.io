Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Just get started – your deadlines are still stored safely. They belong to your browser on this device: if you delete your browser data, they are gone.

What happens after the 30 days?

With Livo Plus everything carries on as before. Without Plus you can still view and delete your deadlines, but you can't add or change any, and reminders stop.

Does heylivo work on iPhone?

Yes. To receive reminders, tap “Share” in Safari, then “Add to Home Screen”, and open heylivo from the new icon.

How does “Hey Livo” work?

Your browser does the speech recognition (in Chrome via Google, in Safari via Apple, for example). Livo only gets the recognised text and works out the type, date and time of the deadline from it. You check everything before it is saved.

Who can see my deadlines?

Only you. The database only ever lets each user see their own deadlines.
